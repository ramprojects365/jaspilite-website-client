How the Jaspilite Referral Program Works

Share the convenience of Jaspilite with your friends and earn rewards for every household that joins.

Step 1

Share Your Invite Code

Open the Jaspilite mobile app, go to your profile, and copy your personal invite code to share via WhatsApp, SMS, or social media.

Step 2

Friend Registers & Saves

Your friend installs the app, inputs your code, and immediately receives RM5 worth of welcome credit points to spend on groceries.

Step 3

You Earn Wallet Points

Once your friend completes their order, your account is credited with reward points that can be deducted from any future grocery cart!
