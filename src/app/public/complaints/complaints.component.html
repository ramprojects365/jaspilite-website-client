Help Center & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about ordering, delivery coverage, payment options, or becoming a vendor? Find answers to the most common inquiries right here.

What is Jaspilite?

Jaspilite is Malaysia's premier on-demand e-commerce platform for ethnic Indian foods, groceries, farm-fresh vegetables, dairy, cooking essentials, and regional dishes. We connect customers across Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Ipoh, Melaka, and beyond directly with top regional grocery stores and restaurants for same-day delivery.

How do I contact the Jaspilite customer support team?

You can reach our friendly support team via phone call or WhatsApp at +60 10 544 9974 Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, including all Malaysian Public Holidays.

Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

You can adjust your delivery address freely inside the mobile app prior to finalizing your checkout. If you need to update the drop-off location after the order has already been placed, contact our support team immediately on WhatsApp at +60 10 544 9974 so we can inform the dispatch driver before they leave the store.

How do I cancel my order?

You can view the "Cancel Order" option in your Order History under your profile. Cancellation is available until the shop keeper begins packing your order. If the button is disabled, please contact the merchant directly (via the phone number on your order screen) or message Jaspilite Customer Care at +60 10 544 9974.

Is there a minimum order requirement for delivery?

Generally, there is no platform-wide minimum order. You can place orders from as low as RM1! A few individual specialty shops may establish a minimum cart threshold of RM30 to RM50 depending on their operational logistics.

How are delivery fees calculated?

Delivery fees depend on the distance between the store and your drop-off address, order weight, and peak demand time. Typically, baseline delivery starts low, with an approximate RM1 per KM fee applied beyond 5KM. Keep an eye out for seasonal Free Delivery promo codes and festive campaigns!

How quickly will I receive my groceries?

Orders are dispatched for same-day delivery! You can choose an immediate express slot or schedule a specific time slot later in the afternoon or evening when you will be home to receive your goods.

What happens if an item is out of stock at the shop?

If an item is unavailable, the merchant or personal shopper calls you directly to suggest an equivalent brand or alternative size. No substitute is finalized without your explicit confirmation.

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD), DuitNow QR, online bank transfer, and direct merchant account settlements. Online debit/credit card gateway integration is also active across participating partner outlets.

How can I register my shop or restaurant on Jaspilite?

Joining Jaspilite has zero upfront setup costs! Simply visit our Become a Partner page or message our vendor support team on WhatsApp at +60 10 544 9974. We will help you list your shop inventory and provide your merchant app within 24 hours.
